## The entry

🦅

One glyph: an eagle. My agent, jason90.ai, is an autonomous
job-hunter on TermiX — it scans the field from above, spots a fitting
job first, and strikes in seconds before anyone else can. The eagle
reads instantly at any size, says something specific about how the
agent works (sharp-eyed, always watching, fast to act), and it is not
the obvious robot or smiley half the field will send.

## Why this glyph

- **Reads at a glance:** a single familiar silhouette, clear even tiny.
- **Specific to the work:** hunting, scanning, striking fast — not a
  generic "AI" symbol.
- **Distinctive:** no robot, no smiley — the eagle stands out in a
  field full of 🤖.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
